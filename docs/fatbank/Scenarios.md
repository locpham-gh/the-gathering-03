# User Scenarios - The Gathering

This document defines the core user journeys of **The Gathering** platform, structured for feature demonstration and technical validation.

---

## Scenario 1: Virtual Space Interaction (Core Gameplay)
*Focus: The immersive experience of moving and interacting in a spatial environment.*

### 1.1: Space Discovery & Entry
**Actor:** Phát — A new user joining a workspace.
- **Context:** Phát receives an invite link to the "Alpha Project Office".
- **Action:** 
    - He clicks the link and enters the "Pre-join" screen.
    - He selects the **"Cyberpunk Scout"** avatar and sets his name.
    - He clicks **"Join Space"** and the engine initializes the map.
- **Outcome:** Phát appears at the spawn point and can see other team members moving in real-time.

### 1.2: Collaborative Interaction
**Actor:** Phát and Minh (Team members).
- **Context:** They need to discuss a design diagram.
- **Action:** 
    - Phát walks his avatar toward the **Meeting Zone**.
    - As he nears Minh, **Spatial Audio** automatically connects their voice bubbles.
    - Phát stands at the **Whiteboard** and presses **'E'** to open the drawing tool.
    - Minh joins the session, and they both draw on the board simultaneously.
- **Outcome:** The team completes the design review without ever leaving the spatial context.

## Scenario 2: Profile & Identity Management
*Focus: Personalization and user persistence.*

**Actor:** Phát — A user wanting to update his professional presence.
- **Context:** Phát wants to change his look for a formal client meeting.
- **Action:** 
    - From the Dashboard, he navigates to **"Profile Settings"**.
    - He uploads a new **Avatar Image** and updates his **Display Name**.
    - He switches his character sprite to the **"Executive"** style.
- **Outcome:** When he joins the next room, all other users see his updated name and professional avatar.

---

## Scenario 3: Space Ownership & Organization
*Focus: Creating and managing collaborative environments.*

**Actor:** Phát — A workspace owner.
- **Context:** Phát needs to organize a town hall meeting.
- **Action:** 
    - He clicks **"Create Room"** and selects the **"Grand Hall"** map.
    - He goes to the **"Events"** tab and schedules a "Product Launch" event for Friday.
    - He creates a new **Forum Topic** in the room to collect questions before the event.
- **Outcome:** A dedicated, structured space is ready for the community, complete with scheduled events and asynchronous discussion threads.

---

## Scenario 4: Administrative Governance (Mainframe Access)
*Focus: Platform stability, statistics, and moderation.*

**Actor:** System Admin — Managing the platform integrity.
- **Context:** The Admin needs to audit platform health and handle user reports.
- **Action:** 
    - **View Statistics**: The Admin opens the **Admin Panel** (Shield icon) to check the **User Presence Density** chart.
    - **User Management**: They search for a reported user and update their status to **"Banned"** to prevent harassment.
    - **Access Control**: They update the **Whitelist** to grant "Partner" status to a specific corporate email domain.
    - **Resource Vault**: They add new books to the **Library Manager** for all users to access.
- **Outcome:** The platform remains secure, updated, and high-performing through active governance.
